Uploader · how updates arrive

Quiet by default: check, download and install without asking, and never during an upload. The proposal from the build agent, plus three additions (marked +).

When it goes wrong
aCan't reach the feedOffline or site down. Tries again in 1 hour, silently. About shows “Couldn't check · Try again”.silent retry
bDownload or signature failsFile deleted, fetched again at the next check. Nothing changes for you.silent retry
cInstall failsThe old version keeps running. About shows “Update failed · Try again · Open log”.old version stays
+ The one loud case
dFeed says: minimum 1.1Only when the site changes so an old uploader can't upload anymore.rare
eUploads pauseSaved data waits on disk. Main window banner “Update needed to keep uploading”, orange dot on the tray icon.visible
fRestart and updateOne click, or it installs at the next safe moment. The waiting upload goes out right after.~2 s
Kept from the proposal
✓Check at start and every 24 hours with the token; download quietly.
✓Install on quit or restart, never during an upload.
✓Settings › About: Check for updates → Checking… → Up to date / Version 1.1 ready · Restart now.
✓No notification for normal updates. Only installed Release builds check.
✓The states are now on the boards (this one, the settings copy and the states board), so they're no longer a deviation.
Added
+Install when WoW is closed. The app starts with Windows and lives in the tray, and most PCs sleep instead of restarting, so “on quit or restart” could take weeks. A setting, on by default.
+Required updates are the one exception to “no notification”: uploads would silently stop otherwise.
+“Updated to 1.1” once in the footer, plus failure states (couldn't check, update failed) and “Restarting after this upload…”.
